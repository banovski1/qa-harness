# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be respectful. Assume the person on the other side is competent and acting in
good faith, and say the technically true thing plainly rather than the socially
comfortable one.

Not acceptable, anywhere in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background
- publishing someone's private information without their permission
- sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to win them, or bad-faith
  argument as a way of wearing people down

Disagreeing with a technical decision is not a violation. How you disagree can be.

## Scope

Anywhere this project is represented: this repository, and any space where you
are acting on its behalf.

## Enforcement

Report to <cvetomirbanovski@gmail.com>. Reports are handled privately, and the
reporter's identity is not disclosed.

The maintainer will respond within 72 hours and may warn, remove content,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban — proportionate to what
happened, with the reason stated.

Enforcement decisions are the maintainer's to make. If you think one was wrong,
say so in the same private channel.
