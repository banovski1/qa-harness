# Contributor License Agreement

**Every pull request must be covered by this agreement before it can be merged.**
The [CLA Assistant](https://github.com/cla-assistant/cla-assistant) bot asks you to
sign it once, on your first PR, by leaving a comment. It never asks again.

## Why this exists

qa-harness is Apache-2.0 and will stay free to use. But under plain
Apache-2.0 every contributor keeps the copyright on their own patch, which means
nobody — including the maintainer — can relicense the project as a whole, and the
IP chain has as many owners as it has contributors.

This agreement fixes that by granting the maintainer the rights to your
contribution *in addition to* the Apache-2.0 licence you already grant everyone
else. **You keep the copyright on your own work.** You can reuse it anywhere.
You are granting a licence, not signing anything away.
